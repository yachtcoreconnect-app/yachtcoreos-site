Advanced Yacht Management Metrics

2026 Focus

RevPAD / RevPAY

Revenue per Available Day/Yacht. Core yield metric combining rate and utilisation.

Occupancy / Utilisation

Booked ÷ available days. Commercial targets often 70%+; typical profile ~10% sailing time.

ADR

Average Daily Rate. Tracked with mid-week vs weekend differentials.

Gross / Contribution Margin

After direct costs. Healthy charter target often >80%.

Crew + Fuel Cost %

Core opex ratio. Aim to reduce from ~120% toward 105% of revenue.

Net Revenue Retention

Growth from existing clients after churn. 110%+ is gold standard.

LTV : CAC

Lifetime value vs acquisition cost. Healthy marine service target ≥ 4:1.

Asset Yield (Carbon-Adj.)

Turnaround + fuel efficiency + carbon intensity under ETS schemes.

OPEX per Vessel Day

Daily running cost vs budget and peers.

Technician Utilisation

Productive hours band typically 75–85%.

Certificate & Drill Compliance

% current certificates, drills on schedule, NCRs closed.

Sailing / Harbour / Anchor

Typical: ~10% sailing, ~56% harbour, ~34% anchor.
